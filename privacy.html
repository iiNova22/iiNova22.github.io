Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Command Center is a personal app used only by its developer. It has no other users.

Google data

  • Google Drive: the app reads and updates files only inside one specific course folder owned by the developer, to keep generated course exports up to date. It does not access other files.
  • Google Calendar: read-only access to the calendars the developer selects, to show events in a daily schedule.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored encrypted in the developer's own private database. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: the developer's support email listed on the Google consent screen.
